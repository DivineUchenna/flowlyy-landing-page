Frequently asked questions

What does the Operating System do?

It runs the quotes, invoices and job admin so your team doesn't have to.

  • Builds quotes from your price list and chases them until you get an answer.
  • Reads supplier invoices, files them and pushes them into Xero or QuickBooks.
  • Chases unpaid invoices politely, on schedule.
  • Logs and tracks service and warranty jobs, and flags them when they're slipping.
Will it send things without my say?

No. Everything is drafted for your approval. It does the typing. You keep the final say.

What does it cost?

You can start with one module.

  • One module: £2,000 (quotes) to £3,500 (money), plus £299/month.
  • Three modules: £6,000, plus £749/month.
  • Full suite: £9,000, plus £999/month.

The full breakdown is in the pricing ledger.

What do I get from the audit?

We walk your operation, tell you what's costing you and rank what to fix. It starts from £1,500 and you keep the plan whether you hire us or not.

If you build with us within 60 days, the audit is credited in full.

Should I book consulting or have you build it?

Book 1:1 consulting if you want to understand it before you buy anything. It's a one-off booking with no retainer and no commitment, covering AI strategy, workflow design, tool selection and team training.

Choose done-for-you if you'd rather have everything done. We build the automations, workflows and AI systems, priced per project. Fixed scope projects run £10,000 to £30,000.

Not sure which? Start with the audit.
