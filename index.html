Frequently asked questions

What does the AI Operating System do?

It runs the parts of your day that eat your evenings.

  • Drafts emails, replies and follow-ups for your review.
  • Pulls research and reports together into one place.
  • Chases invoices, tasks and replies until they're done.
  • Flags anything that's slipping before it becomes a problem.
Will it send things without my say?

No. Everything is drafted for your approval. It does the typing. You keep the final say.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you want off your plate and how much needs connecting. We cover scope and price on the call, once we understand what you actually need.

What do I get from the audit?

We walk through how you work, tell you what's costing you and rank what to fix. You keep the plan whether you work with us or not.

If you build with us within 60 days, the audit is credited in full.

Is this only for businesses?

No. Founders use it to run emails, reports and admin. Professionals and students use it for research, writing and the busywork that isn't actually the hard part of the job.

I already use ChatGPT or Claude. Why do I need this?

A chat window answers one question at a time and forgets it tomorrow. An AI operating system remembers your work, drafts things without being asked, and keeps a queue of what's waiting on you.

Should I book consulting or have you build it?

Book 1:1 consulting if you want to understand it before you commit to anything. It's a one-off booking with no retainer, covering a plan, the right tools, and training for you or your team.

Choose done-for-you if you'd rather have it built. We agree scope on the call, then build the system for you.

Not sure which? Start with the audit.
