Frequently asked questions

What does the Operating System do?

It runs the quotes, invoices and job admin so your team doesn't have to.

  • Builds quotes from your price list and chases them until you get an answer.
  • Reads supplier invoices, files them and pushes them into Xero or QuickBooks.
  • Chases unpaid invoices politely, on schedule.
  • Logs and tracks service and warranty jobs, and flags them when they're slipping.
Will it send things without my say?

No. Everything is drafted for your approval. It does the typing. You keep the final say.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you want off your plate, what you already use and how much needs connecting. We cover the scope and price on the sales call, after we understand the job properly.

What do I get from the audit?

We walk your operation, tell you what's costing you and rank what to fix. You keep the plan whether you hire us or not.

If you build with us within 60 days, the audit is credited in full.

What does the quote actually look like?

Itemised. Materials and labour listed out, priced off your own list, sent the same day. Customers who can see what they're paying for stop shopping the price.

What about a busy week after a storm?

Every call gets answered and booked into your diary in order, and the quotes still go out that day, so nobody's left waiting while you're out making roofs safe.

Will it help my reviews?

It makes sure every enquiry gets a call back and every quote goes out. That's what customers keep mentioning in 5-star roofing reviews.

Should I book consulting or have you build it?

Book 1:1 consulting if you want to understand it before you buy anything. It's a one-off booking with no retainer and no commitment, covering a plan, the right tools, and training for you and the office.

Choose done-for-you if you'd rather have everything done. We agree the scope on the sales call, then build the automations, workflows and AI systems for you.

Not sure which? Start with the audit.
